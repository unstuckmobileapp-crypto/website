FAQ

Common questions

Is Unstuck a replacement for therapy?

No. Unstuck is designed to complement professional treatment, not replace it. The tools are based on evidence-based approaches like ACT and exposure therapy, but we always recommend working with a qualified therapist for personalized care.

Is the app free?

Yes! You can start free: audio sessions, SOS mode, anxiety logging, and the fortnightly anxiety check-in are all available without paying. Unstuck Pro unlocks personalised evaluations, Find Patterns, and unlimited habit and medicine tracking.

What tracking tools are included?

Unstuck includes anxiety logs, an exposure log, interoceptive practice tracking, a validated fortnightly anxiety check-in, a habit tracker, and a medicine tracker. All tools work together — and can be exported as a clinician report — to give you and your therapist a complete picture of your journey.

Is my data private and secure?

Absolutely. Your data is encrypted, stored securely, and never sold to third parties. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app settings.

Can I use Unstuck offline?

Yes. You can download audio sessions for offline listening. Anxiety logs and habit entries are saved on your device and synced when you're back online.

What types of anxiety does Unstuck help with?

Unstuck covers general anxiety, panic attacks, health anxiety, social anxiety, intrusive thoughts, anticipatory anxiety, agoraphobia, driving anxiety, flying anxiety, and insomnia related to anxiety.
